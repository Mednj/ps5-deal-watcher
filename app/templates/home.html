Qualified offers are queued automatically when the watch is active, the offer still qualifies, Telegram is connected, and quiet hours allow delivery. A visible “Needs review” badge can mean descriptive details are uncertain even when your watch qualifies.

Candidate offers need information required by your selected rules, so they are not queued. Name + item-price watches alert on those two criteria only. A candidate’s review label currently does not include a button to confirm it and trigger an alert.

Source checks happen on each source’s schedule and cooldown. “Check now” requests an immediate pass across selected sources; it does not make a restricted source accessible.
